# UnReconcile

[Open in ChatGPT

[Open in Claude

Reconciled Vouchers can be unreconciled without the need for cancellation. Sales/Purchase Invoices, Payment Entries and Journals will have '**UnReconcile'** option available under '**Actions'** drop down.
